# Getting help

- **A result looks wrong** (something is reported as blocked when it isn't, or
  the other way around): open a
  [wrong result report](https://github.com/martinthommesen/nettvern-check/issues/new?template=wrong_result.yml).
- **The program crashes or misbehaves:** open a
  [bug report](https://github.com/martinthommesen/nettvern-check/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml).
- **An idea or feature request:** open a
  [feature request](https://github.com/martinthommesen/nettvern-check/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml).
- **A security problem:** follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

This project can't help with your Telenor subscription or change what Telenor
blocks. Contact Telenor for that.
